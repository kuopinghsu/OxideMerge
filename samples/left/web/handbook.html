Field Service Handbook

This handbook is for field engineers and dispatch coordinators. It describes how a service visit is planned, carried out and closed. Version 3.2, updated on 4 August 2026.

Draft: the regional tables are not final.

1. Before a visit

Every visit starts with a work order in the dispatch system. Read the whole work order before you leave, and call the customer if the access notes are unclear.

1.1 What to bring

  • The standard tool kit, checked against the list inside the lid.
  • Spare parts for the device model:
    • two fuses of each rating;
    • one control board, for models R-200 and R-300 only.
  • A charged tablet with the latest service app.

1.2 Safety checks

  1. Switch the device off and lock the isolator.
  2. Test that the supply is dead with a two-pole tester.
  3. Put up the warning sign.

Never work on a live device. If the isolator cannot be locked, stop and call your team lead.

4. After a visit

Close the work order in the service app before you leave the site. Record the parts you used and the time you spent, and ask the customer to sign on the tablet.

4.1 Terms

First-time fix
A fault that is fixed on the first visit, without a return trip.
Return visit
A second visit to the same fault within 30 days.

4.2 Report template
